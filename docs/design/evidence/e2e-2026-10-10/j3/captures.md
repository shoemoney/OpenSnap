## Notes (J3 rendered matrix)

Rendered evidence only; nothing here is a real-click E2E result. Findings and the full matrix are in `docs/design/e2e/findings.md` (lane J3).

- **File names:** the harness tags each display-option capture's stem: `-rt` Reduce Transparency, `-ic` Increase Contrast, `-dwc` Differentiate Without Color, `-rm` Reduce Motion, joined for combinations (`-rt-ic`, `-rt-ic-dwc-rm`). Each capture's options are in `manifest.json` under `displayOptions`; the options are injected inside the throwaway copy only, no system setting was written.
- **BEFORE column:** twins are looked up in `impl-2026-10-09/audit-fix-3b`; most captures here have none because J3 compares against the baseline of the same build, not against an earlier one.
