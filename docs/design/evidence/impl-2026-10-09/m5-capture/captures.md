## Inspected (round 2, opened at full size)

- **`S29-help-bevel` light:** the editor is at the top of the screen, so the plate sits inside the window: centred, one 12 pt margin under the top bar, over the top of the canvas. The title bar, traffic lights, Hide / Toolbox / Photos / Snap, the ten tools and Color / Size / Font are all uncovered; the plate is fully rounded.
- **`S29-help-bevel-bottom` dark:** a bottom-bar hint (pointer on Wipe): the plate sits one margin above the bottom bar, over the bottom of the canvas; Undo, Wipe, zoom, status and the PNG / JPG, Drag, Upload controls are uncovered.
- **`S29-help-bevel-hanging` light:** with room above the window the plate hangs from its top edge, behind it, its bottom corners joined to the title bar.
- **`S13-picker-no-recording-grant` dark / light:** the hatched lens reads "Screen Recording off" on its plate; the "1028x664" size label is centred under the lens border with a visible gap, the text centred in its own plate.
- **`S13-picker-no-recording-grant-dragging` dark:** same, mid-drag; the selection size read-out "499x300px" is separate from the magnifier label.
- **`S13-picker-no-recording-grant-edge-bottom-right` / `-top-left`:** pointer in the corner of the display; the lens and the label are both fully on screen with the 4 pt gap below the lens border kept (the magnifier is pushed back inside the overlay as one unit).
- **`S16-permission-sheet-in-context` light:** the app-owned sheet is in front of the dimmed editor (rail-less top and bottom bars behind it), title and instructions legible, Open System Settings the accent default and Cancel beside it.

## Behaviour changes in this round (no image can show them)

- **Capture flash preference:** "Show capture flash" (`showCaptureFlash`) off means no flash at any time, with or without Reduce Motion. Reduce Motion with it on plays one 0.05 s frame at the 0.7 peak. This is a deliberate, more intuitive reading of Q5 (which skipped the flash only for Reduce Motion + off). No Settings row yet.

## Known gaps

- **No live Screen Recording:** the magnifier lens is the "Screen Recording off" placeholder (and a synthetic image for `-synthetic-lens`); a real grant was never requested and no real screen content was magnified.
- **No real permission prompt:** the app-owned sheet is shown, but the system's own Screen Recording prompt and the System Settings pane were never triggered.
- **Real flash luminance not measured on a display:** `S15` shows the panel at alpha 0.7 composited by the window server in the capture; peak brightness on a real panel was not measured.
- **Size-label plate on a white overlay:** with no Screen Recording the picker overlay is near white, so the light label plate has almost no edge against it (text is legible). Not changed.
- **Native tooltips and the Share picker** cannot be captured (listed in the manifest gaps).
- **FA mode:** unavailable, SF Symbols only. **Reduce Motion / Increase Contrast** appear only in `S14-countdown-3-contrast-reduce-motion`.
- The BEFORE twins were captured with the previous alert / rail chrome, so pairs are comparable in content, not pixel-aligned; `S15-flash-*` and `S16-permission-sheet` pair with the old `flash-full` / `permission-alert`.
