## Audit fix batch 9 (F-R-001 to F-R-005): what the captures show

Harness captures only (`tools/eye-dump-audit.sh`, throwaway copies, `--icons sf`; Font Awesome Pro is not bundled, so every icon is an SF Symbol or the embedded Free vector). Six sequential runs at `6cc0b37`, every capture with the app active: `base` and `ic` (`--surfaces S03,S11,S17,S23,S42 --sizes 980xmin`, the second with `--display increaseContrast`), `dwc` (`S03,S11,S17,S42`, `--display differentiateWithoutColor`) and `menu1` to `menu3` (`--surfaces S23,S42`, light and dark). 0 gap records in all six; `safety.txt` shows `keyboard 0, buttons 0` in every run (pointer 46 to 156, the parked cursor during menu tracking), and every real-store compare read `RESULT stores unchanged`.

**F-R-002 (Increase Contrast).** Root cause: the tools are two-state buttons, and AppKit's toggle cell drew the template glyph dimmed under the tint (and a disabled cell dimmed it a second time under the 0.38 alpha), so `StateStyles`'s full label colour never reached the screen. The cell no longer dims; Increase Contrast also lifts the disabled alpha from 0.38 to 0.55. Measured from the PNGs (S11 width 980, glyph pixel furthest from its plate, against that plate):

| Glyph | Appearance | before (5b30929, no option / option) | now, no option | now, Increase Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Select tool, unselected | light | 4.00 / 4.00 | 15.1:1 | **21.0:1** |
| Select tool, unselected | dark | 4.57 / 4.50 | 8.4:1 | **10.7:1** |
| Undo, disabled | light | 1.47 / 1.57 | 2.3:1 | **4.8:1** |
| Undo, disabled | dark | 1.63 / 1.78 | 2.6:1 | **4.5:1** |

**F-R-003 (Differentiate Without Color).** `S17-settings-upload-many-*-dwc`: the selected row gains a 2 pt edge in the text colour and a check ahead of the name. Edge against the surface outside the row: light 1.95:1 before, **16.1:1** now (dark 11.1:1 before, 13.2:1 now); against the row's own fill 14.6:1 (light) and 8.2:1 (dark). Rows keep their names aligned (every row reserves the check's slot while the option is on).

**F-R-001 (status notice).** `S42-status-notice-light/dark` show `Saved to History` in all six runs (12 of 12 captures; 0 of 2 dark before), each with a `drawnText` record in its meta (pixels of the label against the label's own rendering of the string: precision and recall 1.0, 0.9 required, first attempt). A scratch build that compares against the wrong string fails both appearances loudly (precision 0.62 to 0.66).

**F-R-004 (Toolbox menu).** 22 Toolbox-menu captures (S23 and S42, light and dark; 3 clean repeat runs of 4 each) and 6 canvas context-menu captures: 0 gaps. One capture (`S42-toolbox-menu-open-light-dwc`) needed the retry (`menuAttempt` 2) and was saved, where the earlier harness would have recorded a gap. `python3 tools/check-eye-dump.py` on this folder: 0 failures, and it now fails a menu gap that no capture explains (self-test cases).

**F-R-005 (input fence).** `safety.txt` prints `keyboard N, buttons N, pointer N` per run and only keyboard, button and scroll events flag a run.
