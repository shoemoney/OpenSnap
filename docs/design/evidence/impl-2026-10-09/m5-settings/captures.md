## What the captures show (review round 2)

The window is built straight from `SettingsWindow.swift` (App.swift is not wired yet), at the screen's own size and again "as sized for a 1280 x 800 screen" (706 pt visible). 36 captures, 0 gaps, real stores unchanged.

- **Tab strip.** The four tabs are an app-owned strip at the top of the content: a gear / paintbrush / crosshairs / cloud-upload icon beside a 20 pt label, 44 pt tall buttons on a 60 pt solid strip with a 1 pt separator below (no glass). The selected tab has the selected fill, a 1 pt accent border and an accent icon. The labels were AppKit toolbar labels at about 13 pt in round 1.
- **General** uses the Font Awesome Free 7.3.1 solid `gear`.
- **Centred forms.** General (640 x 301) and Drawing (640 x 242) size their grid to their own labels and controls and sit in the middle of the window; Capture (640 x 609) and Upload (640 x 400 / empty 286) fill it.
- `S17-settings-capture-error-*` is 657 tall: the refused-shortcut line wraps and the window grows to hold it. Every tab fits the 706 pt screen.
- Short screens (a tab taller than the visible height): the tab scrolls in the content instead of being clipped. That is proven in `SettingsWindowTests` with a fake 500 pt screen, not captured.
- `S18-hotkeys-sheet-*`: the Capture Shortcuts sheet, the same form (760 wide, as tall as its form so Save is in view); `-in-context-*` shows it over the editor.
- The title bar text is OS-owned (it follows the selected tab); it is not part of the 20 pt rule.
- No focus ring is drawn at open: the strip shows one only after a key press moves focus there.
