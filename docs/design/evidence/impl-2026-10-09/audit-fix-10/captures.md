## Audit fix batch 10 (R3-001 to R3-009): what the captures show

Harness captures only (`tools/eye-dump-audit.sh`, throwaway copies, `--icons sf`). Two runs at `9faf5c0`: `--surfaces S46` plain (light and dark) and `--surfaces S46 --display differentiateWithoutColor` (the `-dwc` files). 0 gap records; `safety.txt` shows `keyboard 0, buttons 0` in both runs and `RESULT stores unchanged`. The harness gives the editor the room of a small display (920 × 540 pt, `maximumNormalCanvasOverride`), because this screen is far larger than any snap worth drawing.

- **R3-001** `S46-resize-oversized-unticked-*` and `-ticked-*` (and `-footer-` crops): a 5120 × 3600 snap (2560 × 1800 pt) resized to 1600 wide, above the room. The title reads `1600 × 1125`, the picture is 1600 × 1125, the canvas is shown at 48% by display zoom, the footer has no Original size box, and ticked or unticked is the same picture.
- **R3-002** `S46-corner-drag-*`: the bottom-right corner dragged 150 pt narrower on the same snap; the title reads `618 × 435` and the picture is 618 × 435 (Undo step "Resize Image"), ticked or unticked.
- **R3-003** `S46-export-accessory-footer-ticked-*` and `-unticked-*`: the Export accessory opens with Export at original size ticked when the footer box is ticked, unticked when it is not.
- **R3-007** `S46-upload-selected-not-default-*-dwc`: Example SFTP is selected while the Default pill sits on Example S3. Differentiate Without Color gives the selected row a 2 pt edge and a leading bar; the only check on the list is the Default pill's.
- **R3-008** `S46-capture-note-reregister-error-*`: the Capture note after the shortcut could not be registered again when the window stopped being in use (another claim held the key, `OSStatus -9878`): it carries the error in the error colour instead of "saved as you change them".
- **R3-009** `S46-history-details-deleted-*`: after Delete from Web, Details keeps `1200 × 844 (published)` and marks the address `(deleted)`.

Correction (audit batch 11, R4-006): the snap is 5120 × 3600 pixels (the harness display is 2x), not 2560 × 1800; the `-footer-` shots show the footer, whose size text lives in the title bar; and the `S46-export-accessory-footer-*` shots show "Not available" for the dimensions and encoded size because the harness never called the accessory's refresh. They are recaptured with real dimensions and bytes in `docs/design/evidence/impl-2026-10-09/audit-fix-11/` (`S46-export-accessory-footer-*@e4dcb06`).
