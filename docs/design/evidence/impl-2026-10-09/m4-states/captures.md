## What these captures show, and what they do not

All captures are from one source, `cc97054`, taken by `tools/eye-dump-audit.sh` inside a throwaway copy of the app (never `build/OpenSnap.app` in place), icon mode `sf` (Font Awesome Pro subsets are not bundled here). The machine's accent colour is the system yellow/amber, so every accent fill below is amber, not blue.

* **158 captures at default display options** (S01, S03, S04, S09, S20, S28, S30, S31, S37 and the new S40 component-state surface), light and dark.
* **72 display-option captures**: S03 plus a 16-slug subset of S40, under `-dwc` (Differentiate Without Color) and `-ic` (Increase Contrast), light and dark, window 980 wide. Both options are injected by the harness (`--display`); no system setting was changed. The subset is deliberate: the full S40 set under each option is mostly identical to the default one.
* **S40 state captures** (window 980 wide, Brush selected): tools (selected, hover, pressed, disabled, keyboard focus, selected + focus / hover / pressed), Snap (rest, hover, pressed, disabled, focus), the Size pill (rest, hover, pressed, disabled, focus), the Size popover track (knob hover, knob held, preset hover), the colour swatches (Red selected with its check, Blue hovered, Translucent gray pressed), Undo / Wipe (hover, pressed, disabled, focus), the drag well (rest, hover, pressed, focus), the custom PNG | JPG toggle (PNG, JPG, hover, pressed, focus, disabled) and the canvas border (rest hairline, hover, crop tool). S20 gains one capture: a hovered History tile beside a selected one.
* Hover, pressed and disabled are forced through the entry points the tracking areas, `mouseDown` and `isEnabled` use (`setHovered`, `highlight`, `setPressed`, `isEnabled`); a real pointer cannot be moved in the harness. Keyboard focus uses `makeFirstResponder` in the key window, so those rings are AppKit's real focus rings.

### Gaps

* **Disabled Size popover track**: disabling the pill dismisses its popover, so a disabled track is never on screen; its 0.38 alpha and non-draggable behaviour are covered by `GlassChromeTests` instead of a capture.
* **Disabled drag well**: the well has no disabled state of its own (T6's "plate hidden, notAllowed tooltip" is not built), so there is no capture.
* **Display-option variants** of S01, S04, S09, S20, S28, S30, S31 and S37 were not captured; only S03 and the S40 subset were.
* **Cursor**: the open-hand and closed-hand cursors cannot appear in a window capture; `AppSafetyModernCases` asserts them (`wellCursor`).
* **Focus ring width**: AppKit draws the ring from the focus-ring mask; the 2 pt of T6 is the system's ring, not a width this app sets.
* **Increase Contrast arms**: the harness injects the flag, so state fills use the high-contrast alphas; the system colours inside those arms are the plain light / dark ones, because a headless process cannot build a working high-contrast `NSAppearance`.
* **BEFORE twins**: 146 of 158 default-option captures list a pre-change twin. The other 12 have none (listed below). A twin is the closest pre-change capture of the same control, which is not always the same state: an S40 state that did not exist before (for example a pressed toggle, a selected-and-hovered tool) is paired with the plain capture of that control, and the size pill, drag well and Undo / Wipe states are paired with the bar they sit in. Display-option captures list the twin of the same slug where one exists.
  * S40 without a twin: snap-focus
  * other captures without a twin: S20-history-1120x850-selected, S20-history-1280x800-selected, S20-history-880x600-selected, S20-history-no-match, S28-focus-topbar-snap
