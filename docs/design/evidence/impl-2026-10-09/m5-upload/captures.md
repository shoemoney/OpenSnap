## What the captures show (written from looking at them)

- **Progress (980):** the 20 pt spinner, "Uploading Quarterly plan…" cut at its tail, and one icon-only Cancel (an X); the Original size checkbox is not shown. Everything else in the bar is unmoved.
- **Success (980):** "Link copied" whole, then two icon commands (Open in browser, Copy). There is a wide empty stretch before Save; the bar does not widen.
- **Error (980):** "Error: Uploa…" cut at the tail, then the text commands "Retry" and "Details…". The two text commands have no plate at rest (borderless, like every command here until hover), so they read as plain words; that is a visible weakness. At 1280 the message reaches "Error: Upload to Example S3 failed: the connectio…".
- **Details sheet:** an app-owned sheet titled "Upload failed" with the whole error, one default OK button.
- Light and dark were both captured; dark reads the same, with the spinner and X in the light content colour.
- Uploader is a stub, no network. The "Link copied" state is held for the capture (600 s timer); in the app it lasts 4 s.
- BEFORE twins are the b9319c4-era S21 captures in `docs/design/evidence/2026-10-09` (progress was text only, the URL cut mid-string, the error was a sheet).
